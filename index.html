📖 Guide

❌ Tic Tac Toe

Controls: Tap a cell to place your mark.

Objective: Get 3 of your marks in a row, column, or diagonal before your opponent.

Winning Rules: Play vs AI (Easy/Medium/Hard/Impossible) or a friend in Multiplayer. First to complete a line wins; a full board with no line is a Draw.

Rewards: Win as X vs AI for Remo — Easy +2, Medium +5, Hard +10, Impossible +15. A Draw earns +7 Remo.

🪨 Rock Paper Scissors

Controls: Tap Rock, Paper, or Scissors.

Objective: Beat the AI's pick each round.

Winning Rules: Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, Paper beats Rock. Matching picks is a Draw.

Rewards: +10 Remo for every round you win.

🏓 Pong

Controls: Choose 🤖 1 Player (vs AI) or 👥 2 Player before starting. Drag anywhere on your own half to move your paddle freely - not just left-right, but anywhere within your half. In 2 Player mode both players can drag at the same time on the same device.

Objective: Return the ball past your opponent's paddle to score.

Winning Rules: First player to reach the Win Score wins the match. Adjust the Win Score anytime from the settings row above the table. In 1 Player mode, pick Easy/Medium/Hard/Impossible AI difficulty.

Rewards: Complete Daily Tasks based on Pong points scored for bonus Remo.

🧠 Memory Match

Controls: Tap cards to flip them.

Objective: Find all 10 matching pairs across 20 cards.

Winning Rules: Match all pairs to complete the board; unmatched pairs flip back over.

Rewards: Hints unlock permanently after winning 2 games, +1 Hint every 2 wins after that, plus Daily Task Remo rewards for completed games.

🤖 AI Number Duel

Controls: Pick a secret number, then guess the AI's number using the Higher/Lower/Correct buttons and your own guess field.

Objective: Guess the AI's secret number before it guesses yours.

Winning Rules: Each side has 3 lives. A wrong guess costs a life; the match ends when either side hits 0. Answer Higher/Lower/Correct honestly about your own secret — the AI uses true binary search and will catch impossible answers.

Rewards: Complete Daily Tasks for Duel wins to earn bonus Remo.
